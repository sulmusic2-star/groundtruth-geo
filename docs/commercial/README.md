# Commercial proof package

**Status: prepared locally; not reviewed by an independent evaluator; not sent; no buyer or revenue claimed.**

## What is being sold

Lasting Ground tests address-specific answers produced by government and
property software. For each answer, it asks six plain questions:

1. Did the system use the right property?
2. Did it use the right official source?
3. Does the record support the answer?
4. Does the citation open the record a reviewer needs?
5. Did the system stop when the evidence was not enough?
6. Was the evidence current on the stated date?

The buyer receives the cases, the official evidence, a result for every check,
and a human-readable CITYGRAPH audit. This does not certify a system, replace
professional review, or establish performance outside the agreed cases.

## Send gate

Do not send the outreach drafts as a paid-proof claim until:

- the public evidence branch and 20-case viewer have stable review URLs;
- a separate qualified reviewer checks at least 20 public evidence receipts;
- the reviewer logs every correction and signs a dated review statement;
- a security and data-handling one-pager accurately describes the sole
  proprietor setup; and
- Tim explicitly approves each outbound email.

The first email asks only whether the problem and method fit. Price follows only
after the vendor confirms what it wants tested and what evidence it will accept.
