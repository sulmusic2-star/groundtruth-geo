# Procurement readiness gaps

**Internal checklist. A technically valid benchmark is not automatically ready
for enterprise data, contracts or payment.**

## Must be completed before accepting confidential vendor data

- [ ] Separate business identity and payment details are documented for Tim
  Sullivan as sole proprietor.
- [ ] W-9 information is prepared through an approved private process.
- [ ] A lawyer reviews the evaluation agreement, confidentiality terms,
  limitation of liability and intellectual-property language.
- [ ] Storage location, encryption, account access and backup behavior are
  verified and written down.
- [ ] A short incident-response and breach-notification procedure exists.
- [ ] Data retention and verified deletion are tested.
- [ ] Every external processor is named and vendor authorization is obtained
  before data is sent to it.
- [ ] Professional and cyber insurance needs are quoted and decided.
- [ ] A security questionnaire can be answered with evidence, not guesses.

## Must be completed before calling the method independently reviewed

- [ ] A qualified person who did not build the benchmark reviews 20 public
  cases blind to the gold answers.
- [ ] Corrections and disagreements remain in a dated log.
- [ ] Reviewer qualifications and exact case IDs are disclosed.
- [ ] Public evidence is refreshed if it becomes stale before outreach.

## Must be completed before quoting a fixed paid scope

- [ ] Buyer supplies five representative questions and outputs.
- [ ] Buyer names the product/version and allowed evidence sources.
- [ ] Lasting Ground confirms the six checks can evaluate the outputs.
- [ ] Both sides agree on case count, retest, disputed-case process and delivery
  format.
- [ ] Tim explicitly approves the fee, contract and written purchasing entity.
