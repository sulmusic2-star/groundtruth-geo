# Independent review brief: 20 public cases

**Purpose:** determine whether another qualified person can reproduce the public
gold answers without relying on Lasting Ground's conclusion.

## Reviewer qualifications

Use someone with professional GIS, environmental due diligence, floodplain,
historic-resource, or public-data QA experience. The reviewer must not have
built the benchmark or its evidence refresh code.

## Assignment

For 20 prespecified public cases, the reviewer receives the question, address,
task rule, and official record link—but not the gold answer during first pass.
For each case the reviewer records:

- matched property and confidence;
- official source and qualifying record identifiers;
- yes, no, or unknown under the stated rule;
- any unusable record link or ambiguous rule;
- retrieval date; and
- time spent.

After first pass, compare the reviewer's result with the stored gold. Resolve
disagreements from the official records and keep the original and corrected
values in the log.

## Required statement

The final dated statement must name the reviewer, qualifications, reviewed case
IDs, agreement count, corrections, unresolved cases, and exact limits. It must
not call the benchmark certified, representative, or nationally accurate.

## Spending gate

This brief is prepared only. Hiring or paying a reviewer requires Tim's explicit
approval of the person, fee, and scope.
